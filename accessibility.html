Inclusive Design & Digital Access

Accessibility Statement

Target Standard: Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA

1. Our Commitment

Whimsy Collection is firmly dedicated to ensuring our digital experiences are accessible to all individuals, including people with motor, cognitive, visual, or auditory disabilities. We continually review and optimize our platform against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA.

2. Core Accessibility Features

  • High Contrast Typography: Deep Espresso text (#352820) on Warm Ivory (#F7F2EA) and Pure White surfaces ensures contrast ratios exceeding 7:1, surpassing minimum AA criteria.
  • Keyboard Navigability: All interactive elements—including mega menus, drawer sidebars, category filters, and modal dialogues—are fully navigable and dismissible via standard keyboard (Tab, Enter, Escape).
  • Descriptive Image Alt Text: Every product photograph and category badge across all 87 pieces includes rich alternative text for screen readers.
  • Reduced Motion Support: Interactive VFX sparkle canvases and animations respect OS-level prefers-reduced-motion user preferences.
  • Scalable Typography: Fluid responsive font sizes scale cleanly with browser zoom settings up to 200% without clipping or content overlap.
