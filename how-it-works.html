How WhatsApp Ordering Works

Enjoy personal boutique service, real-time video previews, and hand-delivered jewelry right to your doorstep across Ontario without complicated checkouts or automated bots.

01

Select Your Favorite Pieces

Browse through our online catalog of 87+ pieces or our Instagram profile (@whimsy__collection). Take a screenshot or copy the item name / SKU code (e.g. BR-DRB-200-001).

Tip: You can inquire about single items or curate a complete jewelry set for a special occasion.
02

Connect on WhatsApp at (705) 970-2169

Click any "Inquire on WhatsApp" button or tap our floating green icon. You'll be connected directly with our local boutique team in Ontario. Ask any question about pricing, availability, clasp styles, or wrist fit.

Need to see how it sparkles under sunlight? We gladly send live video clips upon request!
03

Confirm Address & Payment

Provide your delivery street address in Mississauga, Brampton, Toronto, Kitchener, Cambridge, or Guelph. Settle your payment seamlessly via Interac e-Transfer (or arrange cash on local delivery/pickup).

Fast, encrypted, and trusted across Canadian banking institutions.
04

Receive Hand-Delivered Velvet Pouch

Your jewelry is individually polished, packaged inside our signature dusty-rose velvet keepsake pouch with snap button, and hand-delivered safely to your door. We notify you on WhatsApp as soon as your courier is en route!

Ready for gifting right out of the box!
