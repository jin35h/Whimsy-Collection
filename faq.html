Frequently Asked Questions

Find prompt answers regarding our ordering workflow, boutique pricing model, local doorstep delivery, and jewelry care.

As a personal boutique, our goal is to deliver an individualized consultation. Contacting us on WhatsApp allows our stylist to confirm immediate item availability, calculate custom bundle discounts if you purchase multiple items, and provide close-up videos of how the jewelry shines under natural lighting before you commit.

Because Mississauga and Brampton are within our primary daily dispatch routes, in-stock orders are frequently eligible for same-day or next-day direct doorstep drop-off once order details are finalized.

We operate scheduled weekly regional drop days as well as direct regional couriers for the Tri-Cities and Guelph corridor. Delivery transit is typically 24 to 48 hours.

We primarily accept Interac e-Transfer, which is secure, fast, and familiar across all Canadian financial institutions. For verified local contactless drops, cash upon delivery/pickup can also be arranged.

Every single order—regardless of size—is packaged inside our custom dusty-rose velvet keepsake pouch with a golden snap button and royal Whimsy seal. It protects your jewelry from scratches and moisture, makes travel effortless, and is completely gift-ready.

Yes, all of our 316L Surgical Stainless Steel items are naturally hypoallergenic, nickel-safe, and anti-tarnish. For our Fashion Jewelry pieces, we use skin-safe brass/alloy bases with smooth polished finishes. If you have extreme metal allergies, simply message us on WhatsApp and our stylist will guide you to our stainless steel collection.

We personally inspect every clasp, stone, and chain before packaging. If an item arrives with any defect or delivery damage, notify us on WhatsApp at (705) 970-2169 within 48 hours of receipt with a photo, and we will promptly coordinate an immediate replacement or exchange.
