Our Guarantee

The Triple-Inspection Protocol

Before any piece leaves our Ontario atelier for client delivery, it undergoes three rigorous physical checks.

1. Stone & Prong Stability

Every Kundan setting, Polki slice, and crystal facet is individually examined under magnification to confirm firm bezel seatings and eliminate loose stones.

2. Plating Uniformity & Luster

We verify smooth, streak-free champagne gold polish across all curves, joints, and drop suspensions to prevent early discoloration or micro-flaking.

3. Clasp & Tension Testing

Lobster claws, screw posts, push backs, and adjustable dori cords undergo tension cycling to guarantee secure, all-day confidence during celebrations.

E-E-A-T Ethical Transparency

Our Material Authenticity Standards

Whimsy Collection strictly rejects misleading marketing claims. We hold ourselves to Canadian consumer protection guidelines:

  • No Fabricated Gold Karatage: We never advertise fashion jewellery as solid 22k or 24k gold. Our creations are high-grade copper and brass alloys finished in premium champagne gold plating.
  • Lead & Nickel Conscious: We specify alloy blends formulated for sensitive skin comfort. Detailed metal care advice is provided with every order.
  • Authentic Imagery: All 87 pieces in our catalogue are presented with genuine photographs taken from our physical inventory.
