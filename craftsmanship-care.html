Craftsmanship & Jewelry Care

Learn how our materials are selected, how our signature velvet packaging safeguards your investment, and simple steps to keep your jewelry sparkling for years to come.

316L Surgical Stainless Steel

Anti-Tarnish & Waterproof

316L grade stainless steel is renowned for superior corrosion resistance and strength. It does not rust, green your skin, or degrade under daily moisture exposure.

  • Hypoallergenic and nickel-safe for sensitive skin
  • Resistant to sweat and humidity
  • Easy to clean with warm water and micro-cloth

High-Grade Cubic Zirconia (CZ)

Diamond-Like Refraction

We hand-select AAA-grade cubic zirconia stones cut with precision facets to maximize light dispersion. Set in prong, pavé, or bezel settings, they bring stunning festive radiance.

  • Exceptional optical clarity and fire
  • Firmly set to withstand celebration movement
  • Retains sparkle when wiped clean regularly
Care Protocol

5 Golden Rules for Jewelry Longevity

Last On, First Off

Apply perfumes, hairsprays, and lotions before putting on your jewelry. Let chemicals dry completely.

Store in Velvet Pouch

Always return your pieces to the Whimsy rose velvet pouch to prevent exposure to open air and accidental friction.

Avoid Pools & Spas

Chlorinated pool water and hot tubs contain harsh bleaching agents that can strip protective jewelry coatings.

Gentle Polishing

Wipe your jewelry gently with a dry, lint-free microfiber cloth after wear to lift natural skin oils and moisture.
