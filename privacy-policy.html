Compliance & Data Rights

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026 • Compliant with Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)

1. Introduction & Commitment

Whimsy Collection ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website https://whimsycollection.ca. We are dedicated to maintaining the accuracy, confidentiality, and security of your personal information in full compliance with Canadian federal and Ontario provincial privacy laws.

2. Information We Collect

Because we operate as a personalized consultation and concierge atelier rather than an automated transaction engine, we collect personal information solely when you voluntarily provide it:

  • Contact Details: Your name, email address, telephone number, and municipal delivery city in Ontario when you submit an enquiry form or message us.
  • Styling Preferences: Information regarding upcoming wedding dates, event attire, or jewellery design preferences provided during phone or WhatsApp consultations.
  • Technical Data: Basic anonymized analytics such as browser type, general geographical region (e.g., Ontario, Canada), and pages visited to improve website navigation and performance.

3. Purpose of Collection

We use your personal information exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your jewellery enquiries and product SKU availability questions.
  • To coordinate complimentary personal styling consultations.
  • To arrange personalized hand delivery or courier drops within our Ontario service regions (Mississauga, Brampton, Toronto, Kitchener, Cambridge, Guelph).

We never sell, lease, or commercialize your personal information to third-party marketing companies.

4. Security & Retention

We employ physical, organizational, and technological safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. Client enquiry data is retained only for the duration necessary to satisfy the purpose for which it was collected.

5. Your Rights Under PIPEDA

Under PIPEDA, you hold the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate data, or request the deletion of your contact records.
